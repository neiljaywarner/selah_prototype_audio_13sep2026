# Non-Technical Guide & Next Steps: Selah

*For community leaders, pastors, donors, and curious meditators.*

---

## 1. What does Selah do in simple terms?
Imagine you love a specific 4-verse poem from the Bible, like:
> *"Love is patient, love is kind..."* (1 Corinthians 13:4–7)

If you listen to a traditional Bible app, you have to listen to the whole 5 minutes of Chapter 13. When it finishes, it stops. If you're out walking or running, you have to stop, take out your phone, and scrub back to verse 4.

**Selah changes this**:
1. You tap one button: **1 Cor 13:4–7**.
2. It plays *only* those verses with a beautiful human narrator voice.
3. It pauses for a calm 5-second silence so you can pray and reflect.
4. It repeats as many times as you like.
5. It can even switch translations each time it repeats so you hear the words in fresh ways!

---

## 2. How does the technology work without expensive servers?
Instead of paying thousands of dollars every month for heavy cloud servers to chop up audio files, Selah uses an open-source AI called **WhisperX**.
- Before we release an update, WhisperX listens to the narrator's voice and creates a precise map of every verse:
  - *Verse 4 starts at 0:39.6 seconds*
  - *Verse 7 ends at 1:14.2 seconds*
- Your phone simply uses this map like a bookmark to start and stop the audio player at the exact right moment.
