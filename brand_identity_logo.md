# Selah Brand Identity & App Logo Specification

## 🎨 Color Palette
- **Deep Indigo**: `#0F0E26` (Background & Contemplative Dark UI)
- **Vibrant Indigo / Accent**: `#6366F1` (Active Elements & Navigation)
- **Gold Amber / Light**: `#FBBF24` to `#FDE047` (Latin Cross & Highlights)
- **Parchment White**: `#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9` (Open Bible Pages)

---

## 🕊️ Logo Design: Latin Cross & Open Bible
- **Symbolism**: 
  - **Latin Cross**: Centered in radiant gold amber representing divine redemption, meditation focus, and Scripture centering.
  - **Open Book**: Positioned underneath the cross in clean parchment white representing the Holy Scriptures (Berean Standard Bible & World English Bible).
- **Asset Location**: `assets/selah_logo.svg`

---

## 📱 Platform Integration
- **Android**: Generated via launcher icon pipeline from `assets/selah_logo.svg` into `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-*`.
- **iOS**: Configured via `ios/Runner/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/`.
- **Web**: Hosted as favicon and web manifest icon under `web/favicon.png` and `web/icons/`.
