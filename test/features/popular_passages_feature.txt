Feature: Community Popular Passages Discovery
  As a Christian disciple meditating on Scripture
  I want to see what passages fellow believers are contemplating across the community
  So that I can join them in shared meditation without social comparison or gamified leaderboards

  Background:
    Given the Selah Scripture Audio Meditation app is active
    And the user is on the Chapter Player Screen

  Scenario: Browse community popular passages across timeframes
    When the user taps the "🌱 Popular" chip in the horizontal passage row
    Then a bottom sheet opens displaying "Popular Passages"
    And timeframes "This Week", "This Month", and "All Time" are available
    And "This Week" is selected by default
    And beloved passages such as "1 Corinthians 13:4–7" and "Psalm 23" are listed
    And each passage displays reverent communal metrics such as "meditators" and "loops"

  Scenario: Toggle sorting between Meditators and Loops
    Given the Popular Passages modal is open
    When the user toggles the sort order to "Loops"
    Then passages are re-ordered by highest loop completion count
    When the user toggles the sort order back to "Meditators"
    Then passages are re-ordered by highest unique meditators count

  Scenario: 1-Tap meditation playback from Popular Passages
    Given the Popular Passages modal is open
    When the user taps on "Matthew 11:28–30"
    Then the modal closes
    And the player loads "Matthew 11:28–30" into the active meditation engine
    And a "select_popular_passage" telemetry event is logged

  Scenario: Reverence guardrails eliminate competitive ranking
    Given the Popular Passages modal is open
    Then no numeric leaderboard rank badges (e.g. #1, #2) are rendered
    And all passages share peaceful, uniform card borders
    And phrasing emphasizes communal presence rather than competitive virality
